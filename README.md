## Project Overview

The Insight Board is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze ApnoMart's sales and profitability performance during Q2 2026.

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Profit Margin.
- **Sales by Category:** Compares sales across product categories.
- **Sales Trend Over Time:** Shows sales trends during the reporting period.
- **Profit by Region:** Compares profitability across regions.
- **Region Slicer:** Enables interactive filtering by region.

## Key Business Insights

### Sales by Category
Furniture recorded the highest sales at 837,647.85, followed by Clothing at 823,721.30. Electronics recorded the lowest sales at 399,822.20.

**Recommendation:** Maintain appropriate inventory for high-performing categories and investigate opportunities to improve Electronics sales.

### Profit by Region
Central recorded the highest regional profit at 943,677.84, followed by East at 935,706.63. North recorded the lowest regional profit at 795,107.30.

**Recommendation:** Review successful practices in Central and East and investigate North's product mix, discounts, sales, and operating costs.

### Sales by Customer Segment
Corporate customers recorded the highest sales at 4,311,027.50, followed by Small Business at 4,151,641.15. Home Office recorded the lowest sales at 3,750,252.65.

**Recommendation:** Investigate opportunities to improve Home Office sales while maintaining relationships with high-revenue customer segments.
